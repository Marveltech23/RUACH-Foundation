Queen Esther Finishing School

Crowned by Character, Known by Excellence.

About the Programme

Preparation Before Promotion

The Queen Esther Finishing School is the flagship transformational programme of RUACH Foundation for Capacity Building, inspired by Queen Esther's remarkable journey of preparation before promotion. It is designed to equip individuals with the character, confidence, emotional intelligence, communication skills, etiquette, and sense of purpose needed to flourish in every sphere of life.

Open to both women and men, the programme is founded on the belief that lasting influence begins with inner transformation. Through mentoring, coaching, practical learning, and immersive experiences, participants are empowered to become people of character, purpose, and influence.

Whether you are preparing for higher education, the workplace, entrepreneurship, marriage, leadership, or public service, the Queen Esther Finishing School provides a transformational journey that helps you discover your potential, build your capacity, and maximise your impact.

Our Certificate Programmes

A Progressive Journey of Transformation

The Queen Esther Finishing School is delivered through three four-week certificate programmes, each carefully designed to develop the whole person. While each may be taken independently, together they build confidence, cultivate character, and empower purposeful influence.

Duration · 4 Weeks

Certificate in Royal Identity

Awakening Confidence, Worth & Purpose

Every transformation begins with identity. This programme helps participants discover who they are, recognise their unique value, and build the confidence to live intentionally through self-awareness, resilience, and purpose discovery.

Duration · 4 Weeks

Certificate in Royal Excellence

Cultivating Character, Presence & Excellence

Character is the true mark of distinction. Participants develop communication, etiquette, emotional intelligence, professionalism, and leadership behaviours — learning to carry themselves with grace, integrity, and excellence.

Duration · 4 Weeks

Certificate in Royal Influence

Empowering Vision, Wisdom & Impact

True influence begins with purposeful living. This programme equips participants to lead with wisdom, communicate with impact, and use their gifts to serve their families, workplaces, and communities.

The Culmination

The Crowning Experience

The Queen Esther journey culminates each year in The Crowning Experience — our annual celebration of transformation. More than a graduation ceremony, it is a commissioning of individuals who have embraced the values of character, purpose, and excellence.

The crown is not a reward for perfection, but a symbol of the personal growth, responsibility, and influence developed throughout the journey. Together, our three certificate programmes and The Crowning Experience provide a complete transformational pathway.
